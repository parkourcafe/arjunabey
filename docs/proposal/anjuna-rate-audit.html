What we would do

Anjuna Bay

First thirty days — rate work only, no advertising budget

Everything below is done inside the extranet and the channel manager. None of it requires spend.

Weekend premium

Friday and Saturday priced above midweek across the full calendar. Start at 15–20% and test.

Depth-based rate ladder

Further-out dates priced lower, rate rising as the calendar fills. Today the spread between near and far dates is about 15%.

Minimum stay on peak dates

Protects high-value periods instead of leaving one-night gaps in the calendar.

Orphan-night rule

A two-night minimum means a single free night between bookings cannot be sold at all. An exception rule releases those dates instead of losing them.

Early-booking rate

Applied to the months that currently carry no forward demand at all.

Airbnb rebuild and channel parity

Eight months with no bookings on Airbnb is not a demand problem. Sync, pricing, photography and titles reviewed listing by listing against what works on Booking.

What we cannot see from outside

  • Realized rate and actual occupancy
  • Whether a blank date is booked or simply unsellable
  • Owner blocks and closed dates
  • Promotions and discounts applied
  • Channel mix and real commission
  • Cancellations and no-shows

One report closes all of it

Monthly, per unit, last 12 months: nights sold, revenue, platform commission, channel.

From that, realized rate, occupancy, RevPAR and channel mix calculate themselves — with no assumptions from us.
